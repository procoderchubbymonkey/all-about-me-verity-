six stories, six evenings

Every good love story
leaves a little ache behind.

This is a running log of the romance anime that have stayed with me — not always the loudest or the happiest, but the ones that felt honest. Some are sweet, some are bittersweet, and a couple will quietly wreck you. Scroll down for the full list.

01

Ride Your Wave

film · grief & love · ocean-soaked

A new girl in town falls for a firefighter, and just as the romance gets good, tragedy rewrites everything. What's left is a story about griveing.

Why it stuck with me: i cried at 3 in the morning after watching it.

02

Your Lie in April

tv series · music · cherry blossoms

A pianist who lost his ability to hear his own music meets a violinist who plays like she's daring the world to keep up. It's about being brought back to life by someone else's noise — and what it costs.

Why it stuck with me: it makes you think everything is perfect then it goes off the rails.

03

My Happy Marriage

tv series · arranged marriage · slow warmth

A girl who's never been shown kindness is married off to a man with a cold reputation — and instead finds the first real gentleness of her life. It's a quiet, old-fashioned kind of romance: patient, healing.

Why it stuck with me: it is a heart warming story with an actual happy ending.

04

I Made Friends with the Second Prettiest Girl in My Class

manga · yuri · school life

A plain, awkward girl gets pulled into orbit around her school's most popular girl — and what starts as one sided admiration slowly, nervously, becomes something mutual. Soft and a little chaotic, in a good way.

Why it stuck with me: it is funny as it is quircky and has heart warming moments.

05

Horimiya

tv series · opposites attract · comfort watch

Two classmates discover each other's secret home lives and end up seeing the real version of one another before anyone else does. Low-drama, high-comfort — the kind of romance you rewatch when you need a good mood.

Why it stuck with me: it lets a couple actually be happy without punishing them for it.

06

Smoking Behind the Supermarket with You

manga · will-they-won't-they · melancholy & rain

Sasaki, a worn-out 45-year-old salaryman, gets through his days on cigarettes and the cheerful face of Yamada, his favorite cashier. When he can't find her one night, he meets the cool, teasing Tayama in the smoking area out back — never realizing she and Yamada are the same person. Quiet, a little melancholy, and surprisingly tender.

Why it stuck with me: it shows romance animes can center around adults and not just teenage highschoolers and i love the quiet calm vibe.

If you've watched or read any of these, I'd genuinely love to hear what you thought. And if you haven't — do me a favour and take a watch and come back to me.
